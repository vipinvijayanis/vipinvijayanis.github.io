Checkout Conversion Dashboard · Launched April 2026

A checkout view everyone could use.

From March 2026, the checkout team had no dedicated data science support. Existing funnel reporting was outdated and locked inside licence-restricted analytics tools.

I built an HTML-based dashboard that anyone in the company could access, keep current and shape around the questions the team needed to answer—without the visual limits of existing tools.
